DM1 six-step analysis: executive summary

1Understand2Split / Cross-validation3Preprocess4Select5Train6Evaluate
Per-arm analysis of the simulated DM1 trial following the kickoff six-step workflow. Genuine 80/20 stratified split (seed 42) per arm, test set scored once. Two questions: which covariates carry signal, and does ML improve on covariate modelling.

The six-step flow

  1. Step 1: Understand – Explore structure, distributions, correlations (train only)
  2. Step 2: Split / Cross-validation – Hold out a locked test set per arm; repeated CV inside train
  3. Step 3: Preprocess – Encode and scale only what each method needs; fit on train only
  4. Step 4: Select – Let data traits drive the choice of methods
  5. Step 5: Train – Fit and tune on the training set only; CV scoreboard
  6. Step 6: Evaluate – Score the locked test set once; covariate vs ML; signal vs noise

Covariate identification: signal vs noise (one rule)

SIGNAL = all 4 criteria; BORDERLINE = 2 or 3; NOISE = 0 or 1. Applied unchanged to every arm and both outcomes. Criteria: (a) selection frequency >= 0.6 over LASSO / elastic net / stepwise-BIC bootstraps; (b) sign stable in >= 4/5 subsamples; (c) used by >= 2 of 3 method families; (d) beats a shuffled-outcome null. Train-only evidence; details and ranked tables in Step 6.5.

No consistent advantage for ML. It is ahead in 2 of 6 test comparisons: Tideglusib continuous (RMSE 2.35 vs 2.83, the clearest, supported by CV) and Oligonucleotide label (AUC 0.800 vs 0.771, within noise). It ties or trails in the other four, and all interval pairs overlap.

Top caveats

  • Test sets are 33-34 patients (10-16 responders): wide CIs, one patient moves a score.
  • Data are simulated; nothing here describes real patients or therapies.
  • The responder label gives little signal (1.9-3.2 responders per model column).
  • Permutation importance at small n is noisy and shares credit among correlated covariates; SIGNAL calls are candidates for confirmation, not proof.
  • The main project had scored this split once before with different frozen specifications; that file was not used here. Selection in this pipeline was CV-only.
