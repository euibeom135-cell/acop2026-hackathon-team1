6.5 Covariate identification: signal versus noise

This is the deliverable on which the kickoff checklist asks ('key covariates are separated from noise'). It uses one rule, fixed in advance and applied unchanged to every arm and outcome; all evidence is from TRAIN rows only (selection frequency over bootstrap resamples, sign stability over subsamples, multi-family use, and a shuffled-outcome null). The test set plays no part in any verdict.

The four criteria (one point each)

Criterion Definition (train rows only)
a Selection frequency pooled selection frequency >= 0.6 over three selectors (LASSO lambda.min, elastic net, stepwise-BIC) in 200 bootstrap resamples
b Sign stability sign of the lm/glm coefficient equals the full-train sign in >= 4 of 5 train subsamples (80% each)
c Multi-family use used by >= 2 of 3 families on full train: penalised (LASSO or EN non-zero); stepwise-BIC retained; nonlinear/ML (GAM term p < 0.05, or RF held-out permutation importance > 0 in >= 4/5 folds)
d Beats shuffled-outcome null lm/glm |t| above the 95th percentile of its own null (500 outcome permutations), or RF held-out permutation importance above the 95th percentile of the RF null (100 permutations)

6.6 Conclusions and caveats

  • Signal covariates are few: Mexiletine myotonia severity and disease duration (continuous); Oligonucleotide log10 CTG (continuous); Tideglusib cognitive function (both outcomes) and disease duration (continuous). The responder label gives SIGNAL only for Tideglusib cognitive function; elsewhere label evidence is BORDERLINE at best.
  • Prediction: every CV-picked model beat its baseline in train CV; on test the covariate and ML tracks are largely indistinguishable, with ML clearly ahead only for Tideglusib continuous.
  • Small test sets (33-34 patients, 10-16 responders): intervals are wide, one patient moves a classification score, and gaps of a few hundredths should not be ranked.
  • Simulated data: results describe a synthetic dataset, not real patients or approved therapies.
  • Label outcomes yield little signal: the dichotomised label has fewer effective observations per covariate (1.9-3.2 responders per column), so selection frequencies are lower and many covariates are BORDERLINE.
  • Permutation importance at small n is noisy and splits credit between correlated covariates (Tideglusib disease duration is SIGNAL on train but absent from the top 3 test importances of the XGBoost pick, where cognitive function dominates), so importance rankings are supporting, not decisive.
  • Verdicts are train-only evidence; a covariate marked SIGNAL here is a candidate for confirmation in independent data.
